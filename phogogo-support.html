Support resources

If you are experiencing issues with the app, please try the following:

  • Photo access: phogogo asks the first time you open it. If you later want to allow access, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → phogogo.
  • iCloud photos: A round can download originals before you decide. Turn off “Download from iCloud” on the home screen if you would rather decide from the thumbnail.
  • Deleted photos: They go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. iOS removes them after 30 days, and that is when the space comes back.
  • Restart the app: Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.
